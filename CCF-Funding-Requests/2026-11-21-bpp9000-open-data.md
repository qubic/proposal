## Summary
- **8,000 USD** to dk.dev, a Qubic core developer, for **bpp9000-reader**: a service that reads BPP-9000 straight from the nodes, built in four stages over two months. Half is paid at the start, half after the stages are accepted.
- **2,000 USD** to Qubic Church to keep the public BPP-9000 site running for two months and add prediction-mode views, the reader, an epoch archive and alerts. A year of that site was built unfunded.

## Why It Matters
- **Transparent.** Today almost every public BPP-9000 number comes from one explorer. The reader adds a second source, and the site flags any hour where the two disagree.
- **Easy to use.** Anyone can follow the experiment hour by hour in a browser, with no node, wallet or code.
- **Ready for prediction mode.** Built now, it works on the day official calls go live ([qubic/core #1072](https://github.com/qubic/core/issues/1072)), and keeps an archive across epochs, which start from zero.
- **Visibility for Qubic.** Share cards, a public archive and checkable data give writers and researchers something concrete to link to. We know of no other chain with an open, hourly, recomputable record of a decentralised AI.

## The Work
| Stage | What dk.dev builds | Accepted when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hour outcomes | Oracle price and target for every hour, read from the node | A full epoch matches the node and Binance BTC/USDT 1h |
| 2. Each computor's call | Rebuilds every network from public tick data, scores it bit-exact with core v1.313.0, stores its call before the hour starts | Every rebuilt score equals the score the network accepted |
| 3. The service | Unattended, multi-node, read-only JSON API, one file per closed epoch | Seven days with no missing hour |
| 4. Docs and proof | README and a script that recomputes any hour from the network alone | An outsider reproduces one hour with the README alone |

No seeds, keys or operator signatures are needed. The repository is private while it is built; computors can ask to review it.

### Qubic Church ([qubic.church](https://qubic.church))
A year of work, paid for ourselves. Many people first see BPP-9000 here.

**What is there now.** We add pages as epochs and core releases come out. Everything is free, with no account needed:

- **BPP-9000, live.** The [terminal](https://qubic.church/bpp9000/terminal) shows every hour's computor votes and two scores. [Brain Watch](https://qubic.church/bpp9000/brains) rebuilds real mining networks in the browser and replays their hours: 26 of 26 checked hours match core v1.313.0. [Science](https://qubic.church/bpp9000/science) explains what the experiment tests, with Come-from-Beyond's words quoted and dated.
- **A journal for every participant.** People join under a handle with one key and no personal data, make their own calls and keep a public record: 68 participants as of 21 November.
- **Network tools.** [Is Qubic down?](https://qubic.church/status), the [network monitor](https://qubic.church/monitoring), [Epochs](https://qubic.church/epochs) (the week in Aigarth) and [WEN](https://qubic.church/wen), every date that matters, each with its source.
- **Reading.** Two books, *Qubic: The Long Version* (18 chapters, about 81,000 words) and *Come-from-Beyond*; [The Road to The Day](https://qubic.church/timeline); plain-English guides to Qubic, Anna, mining and getting QUBIC.
- **Community.** The Crypt, an open forum with 29 long reads by community authors; Maria, our AI on X; share cards so results travel outside Discord.
- **Research.** A public [research log](https://qubic.church/docs) and the Anna Matrix explorer, every number recomputed, with what is not proven marked as such.

**What it costs.** Writing, research, design (including a paid designer) and code, plus hosting: Vercel Pro (20 USD a month plus usage) and an Upstash Redis store on pay-as-you-go. Traffic from BPP-9000 pushed the store past its free plan in November. Qubic Church has never received CCF funding; our epoch 239 request did not reach the vote minimum.

**What the 2,000 USD buys in the next two months.**

- **Keeping it running:** hosting and the database for the terminal, Brain Watch and the journal, through the start of prediction mode.
- **Prediction-mode views:** the core's official hourly calls next to the training votes, from the first day they go live.
- **The reader on the site:** a second, independent source on every hour, with a visible flag wherever the explorer and the reader disagree.
- **An epoch archive:** every closed epoch's hours kept on the site, since the networks start from zero each epoch.
- **Brain Watch on official calls:** replaying the networks behind real, recorded calls.
- **Hourly best-network record:** which network led each hour and what it said, kept for everyone to see.
- **Alerts:** a simple way to follow each hour's call and result without opening the site.

So far we have built this without funding. The grant keeps it running for two months and adds what BPP-9000 needs next.
